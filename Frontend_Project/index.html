ABOUT / 01THE PERSON BEHIND THE SCREEN

01

I don't just build things.
I think about how they should feel.

I'm Vishakha Hinge — an Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering student with a mind that naturally moves between technology and design.

While engineering taught me how things work, design taught me to ask a different question: “Could this be better?”

I enjoy taking an ordinary idea and giving it structure, personality and purpose — whether that's turning a blank Figma canvas into an intuitive interface, transforming information into a presentation people actually want to read, or shaping a technology project into something people can genuinely use.

My strongest space is visual and UI/UX design. I'm comfortable with Figma, Canva and MS Office, especially when it comes to creating interfaces, presentations, reports and visual experiences that feel clean without feeling lifeless.

On the technical side, Python and IoT are where I enjoy building. I don't see design and technology as two separate worlds — I like standing somewhere in between them.
